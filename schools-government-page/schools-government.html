Understand the System

How Security Window Film Works

Security film uses multiple layers of durable polyester applied to existing glass. When glass breaks, the film can help hold fragments together. For certain forced-entry applications, a compatible attachment system may be specified to help retain the filmed glass within the frame.

  • Helps hold shattered glass together
  • Can delay access through broken glazing when appropriately tested and installed
  • May reduce hazards from flying glass fragments
  • Can be selected for impact and blast-related glazing requirements

Security window film is not bulletproof, does not make glass unbreakable, and cannot guarantee prevention of forced entry or injury. Performance depends on the entire glazing system and applicable test standards.
